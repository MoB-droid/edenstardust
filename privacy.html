Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information edenstardust.com ("EdenStardust", "we") collects when you use the site, and how it is used.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile (name and profile picture) from Google.
  • Password: if you sign up with email, your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our login provider. We never see it.
  • Login activity: the date and time of each sign-in and the sign-in method (email or Google).

2. How we use it

  • To let you create an account, sign in and stay signed in.
  • To show the site owner a list of who has signed in and when.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase stores accounts and login activity on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files.

4. Cookies and local storage

The site stores a sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and login activity until you ask us to delete them.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing rayonraayon@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
